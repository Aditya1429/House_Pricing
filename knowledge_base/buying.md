# Buying Guide and RERA

## What is RERA?
RERA (Real Estate Regulation and Development Act, 2016) requires builders to register projects with the state authority, disclose approvals and timelines, keep 70% of buyer funds in a separate account, and compensate buyers for delays. Karnataka RERA and MahaRERA both offer online project search.

## How do I check if a project is RERA registered?
Find the RERA registration number in the builder's advertisement or agreement, then search it on the state portal: rera.karnataka.gov.in for Bangalore, or maharera.maharashtra.gov.in for Mumbai and Pune. Check the approved plan, completion date and any complaints filed.

## What documents should I verify before buying?
Verify the title deed and chain of ownership for at least 30 years, the encumbrance certificate, approved building plan, occupancy certificate (OC) and completion certificate, khata (Bangalore) or property card / 7/12 extract (Maharashtra), property tax receipts, society NOC for resale, and RERA registration for new projects. Have a property lawyer review these.

## What is the difference between carpet, built-up and super built-up area?
Carpet area is the usable floor area inside the walls. Built-up area adds wall thickness and balconies. Super built-up area adds a share of common areas such as lobbies and lifts, and is typically 25 to 40% larger than carpet area. RERA requires builders to quote carpet area. PropertyLens estimates assume the commonly quoted saleable (built-up) area.
