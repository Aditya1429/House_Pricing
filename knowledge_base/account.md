# Account, Privacy and Support

## Do I need an account?
No. Estimates, the Price Assistant and all calculators work without signing in. Saved comparisons are kept only for your current browser session.

## Is my personal data stored?
Estimates you run are not stored. If you raise a support ticket, we store your name, email and message so our team can reply. Feedback on assistant answers is stored without personal details. We never sell personal data.

## How do I delete my data?
Raise a support ticket with the category "Privacy" and the email you used. We delete ticket records within 30 days of a verified request.

## How do I contact support or talk to a person?
Use the Help & Support page and choose "Talk to a person" to raise a ticket. You will get a ticket ID straight away. Our team replies by email within 2 business days. You can check a ticket's status any time on the same page using its ID.
Keywords: contact support email phone customer care help desk ticket status

## How do I report a wrong estimate?
Raise a support ticket with the category "Estimate feedback" and include the property details and the price you believe is correct, with a source if possible. This helps us improve the model.
