# Stamp Duty, Registration and Purchase Costs

## What is the stamp duty in Bangalore?
In Karnataka (Bangalore), stamp duty is 5% of the property value for properties above ₹45 lakh (lower slabs apply below that), plus a 1% registration fee. Small cesses may apply on top.

## What is the stamp duty in Mumbai?
In Mumbai, stamp duty is about 6% of the property value including the 1% metro cess. Women buyers get a 1% concession. Registration is 1% of the value, capped at ₹30,000.

## What is the stamp duty in Pune?
In Pune, stamp duty is about 7% of the property value including local body and metro cesses (varies by municipal area). Registration is 1% of the value, capped at ₹30,000.

## What other costs should I budget for when buying?
Besides stamp duty and registration, budget for GST on under-construction property (5%, or 1% for affordable housing; none on ready-to-move resale), brokerage of about 1 to 2%, legal due diligence fees, society transfer charges, maintenance deposits, and interiors. A safe rule is to keep 8 to 12% of the property price aside for these costs.
