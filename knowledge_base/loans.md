# Home Loans and EMI

## How is EMI calculated?
EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12 and by 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments. The Predict page includes an EMI calculator.

## How much home loan can I get?
Banks usually cap total EMIs (including existing loans) at 40 to 55% of net monthly income, known as the FOIR (fixed obligation to income ratio). They also finance at most 75 to 90% of the property value depending on loan size, so you need a down payment of 10 to 25%. ## What home loan interest rates should I expect?
Home loan rates in India are typically between 8.25% and 9.75% per year for salaried borrowers with good credit scores, linked to the RBI repo rate. Rates change, so confirm with lenders before deciding.

## What tax benefits are available on a home loan?
Under the old tax regime, interest up to ₹2 lakh per year on a self-occupied home is deductible under Section 24(b), and principal repayment up to ₹1.5 lakh qualifies under Section 80C. The new tax regime does not allow these deductions for self-occupied homes. Consult a tax adviser for your situation.
